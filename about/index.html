About us

We started Yusha Cleaning because booking a cleaner in Houston meant phone tag and a price you only learned at the door.

How we work

Every job is priced from one published list. The ticket you build on this site is the price you pay, and the card is charged only after the work is done.

Cleaners in our network are independent professionals. Each is interviewed, background-checked and insured before their first job, and rated after every visit. They keep the larger share of every booking.
