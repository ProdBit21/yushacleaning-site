Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Versioned Systems, LLC, doing business as Yusha Cleaning, collects, uses and protects personal information when you visit our website or book a cleaning.

Last updated October 27, 2026

1. What we collect

  • Contact details: name, email address and phone number.
  • Service details: address, home size and rooms, extras, preferred dates, notes, and access instructions such as door or lockbox codes.
  • Account details, if you create an account: login email and an encrypted password.
  • Payment records: amounts, dates and PayPal transaction ids. PayPal handles your card or PayPal login; we never receive or store your full card number.
  • Messages and applications you send us, including job applications from Cleaners.
  • Website usage: pages viewed, device and browser type, approximate location from your IP address, and how you found us, collected through cookies and similar tools (see “Cookies and analytics”).

2. How we use it

To price, schedule and carry out your cleaning; to send confirmations, reminders, arrival updates, receipts and payment links; to process payments and refunds; to answer messages and handle complaints, guarantees and claims; to prevent fraud and keep Cleaners safe; to improve our website and services; and, only if you opt in, to send occasional offers.

3. Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share only what is needed with:

  • the Cleaner assigned to your visit: your name, address, phone number, the booking details and access instructions;
  • PayPal, to process payments and refunds;
  • service providers that run our website, booking system, email and text messages, and analytics, under contracts that limit their use of the data; and
  • authorities or others when required by law, or to protect the safety and rights of customers, Cleaners or the business.

4. Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand which pages help people book, using cookies that measure visits in aggregate. Google’s use of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can block cookies in your browser settings or install the Google Analytics opt-out add-on; the site keeps working either way.

5. How we protect it

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access instructions are stored encrypted and shown only to the Cleaner assigned to your job. Staff and Cleaners see only what their role needs. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your information, we will tell you as Texas law requires.

6. How long we keep it

We keep booking and payment records for as long as tax and accounting rules require, typically seven years. Access instructions are removed when you ask or when you close your account. Unsuccessful job applications are deleted after two years.

7. Your choices and rights

You can ask to see, correct, download or delete your personal information, or to stop marketing messages, by emailing inquiry@yushacleaning.com. Reply STOP to any text to stop texts. We will answer within 45 days and will not treat you differently for asking. We may keep records we must retain by law.

8. Children

Our services are for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

9. Changes

We will post any change to this policy here with a new “Last updated” date, and email customers about material changes.

10. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: inquiry@yushacleaning.com or (832) 526-0373.
